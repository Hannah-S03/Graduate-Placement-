What is saved, and where

The hub lets you enter your own information: your name, role and start date, goals, milestones, skill ratings, pinned and saved items, contacts, weekly reflections and achievements, and optionally your email address (only used to fill in the “Email me this” draft). The round AI agent button works out your week ahead from this saved information, on your device, using fixed rules: nothing is sent to an AI service. It also remembers where you last put the button. You can choose to share a progress summary with the programme team: you download a file, see exactly what is in it, and send it yourself. The programme team can also send you a programme update file with messages and events, which you choose whether to import: it holds no personal data about you, and it only adds messages to My profile and events to your calendar after you confirm. The programme team can also send you a goals file, which you choose whether to import on My journey: it only adds or updates goals you can see in a preview first, and never overwrites your own tasks, progress or comments. You can choose to include your goal titles, your weekly progress updates, your weekly goals and your weekly reflections (all off unless you tick them). It never includes your 1:1 notes, feedback log, contacts, career interests, email address or achievement details, and nothing is sent unless you send it. All of it is saved only in your own web browser, on the device you use. It is not sent to a server, to the programme team or to your manager, and nobody else can see it.

What this means for you

  • Your information does not follow you to another browser or device.
  • Clearing your browser's site data or cookies can delete it. Use Download my data on My profile to keep a copy, and Restore from a file to load it again.
  • Anyone who can use your browser profile on your device may be able to see it. Do not use the hub on a shared computer unless you reset it afterwards.
  • The contacts you add are other people's details stored on your device. Only add what you need.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses no analytics and sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember what you enter.

Deleting your information

Use Reset everything on My profile, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

If you contact us

If you email the programme team, we use your email address and message only to reply to you (retention period to confirm).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any information we hold about you. Contact us using the details on the help page.
