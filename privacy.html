What we collect

The details you give on the application form: contact details, education, eligibility, your CV or a link to it, and your written answers. Optional equal-opportunities questions are collected separately and are not shown to people reviewing your application.

Why we collect it

To assess your application, contact you and, if you are successful, arrange your placement.

Where it is stored

The application form is a Google Form hosted by Google. Responses are stored in an organisation-owned Google account and are visible only to named reviewers.

How long we keep it

Unsuccessful applications are deleted after 6 months (to confirm).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Contact us using the details on the contact page.
